CalEyeZ

Automated nutritional analysis via sensor fusion — dual-model food recognition + BLE gravimetric weight + USDA nutrient lookup.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering B.Sc. Final Project · Shenkar · Raz Dvora & Roi Tzur · Supervisors: Dr. Gabriela Dorfman Furman, Dr. Zeev Weissman

Living document. Single source of truth for the whole project. Sections are marked done / wip and updated as work progresses.

1 · Overview

CalEyeZ removes the friction from nutrition tracking: place food on a scale, take a photo, and receive a calorie + macronutrient breakdown automatically. It solves two independent engineering problems:

  • "What?" — identifying the food, including local Israeli dishes that standard models miss, without catastrophic forgetting of international foods. Solved with a hybrid Global + Local model pair routed by an arbiter.
  • "How much?" — obtaining an accurate weight. Solved by reading the mass directly from a reverse-engineered BLE scale, after vision-based volume estimation was evaluated and rejected (§7).

2 · Architecture

📷 Camera→ Global model (132)+ Israeli model (13)→ XGBoost Router→ class
⚖️ BLE Scale→ weight (g)→ Fusion (class × weight)→ USDA API→ kcal + macros

Edge-computing desktop application (Python). Inference runs locally on an NVIDIA GPU; only the nutrient lookup touches the network.

5 · Israeli Model done

YOLO11l-cls, 13 Israeli classes (baklava, bourekas_cheese, falafel, hummus, jachnun, malawach, meorav_yerushalmi, sabich, samosa, schnitzel, shakshuka, shawarma, sufganiyah). Run: runs/israeli_food_yolo11l/, imgsz 224. Strong on its own domain (~93%) where the Global model scores 0% — this validates the specialized model.

Known behaviour: the Israeli model is overconfident on out-of-domain food (it can fire >0.9 on a non-Israeli image), so a naive confidence gate cannot route domains. This is the core difficulty the arbiter must learn around.

6 · Arbiter / Router wip

The router decides, per image, which expert to trust — enabling local-cuisine coverage without retraining or degrading the Global model (solving catastrophic forgetting). Its value is coverage, not raw accuracy: a perfect router caps system accuracy at ~88.4% (the Global model's own ceiling on ~93% of the data).

Training-data generation

Generator: scripts/arbiter/generate_arbiter_dataset.py. Both models run over the held-out val+test images of both domains (~32k images) — train images are excluded because the Global model overfits them, which would miscalibrate the confidence features. One row per image:

image_path, domain, true_class,
g_top1..g_top5, g_conf1..g_conf5, g_entropy, g_margin, g_correct,
i_top1..i_top5, i_conf1..i_conf5, i_entropy, i_margin, i_correct,
winner   (global / israeli / none)

7 · Weight Estimation — Approaches Evaluated & Rejected wip

Determining food mass from a photo was investigated thoroughly before the final design. Each vision approach failed for a specific, measured reason, motivating the pivot to a direct hardware reading.

ApproachPrincipleWhy rejected
Coin fiducial + HoughCircles₪1 coin (18 mm) detected → pixels→cm scaleCalibrates the 2-D plane only; no object height
MiDaS depthMonocular relative depth → integrate to volumeScale-ambiguous, noisy on small objects; needs fiducial every shot
Shadow geometryheight = Lshadow · tan(α), α = light angleDepends on a known light-elevation assumption; lighting-sensitive
Volume → massmass = volume · densityDensity problem: fluffy vs. dense foods of equal volume differ greatly
OCR of scale displayRead the 7-segment readout (Tesseract/EasyOCR)Glare/angle/lighting fragility on the LCD
BLE (final)Read the weight packet at the sourceSelected — deterministic, robust, no vision error

Ground-truth validation (Archimedes)

Vision volume estimates were validated against water-displacement (true volume). Example (apple, ₪1 fiducial): MiDaS pipeline estimated 139.3 cm³; Archimedes displacement (300 ml → [TODO: final ml]) gives the true volume. [TODO: error table — apple & can: estimated vs true volume, % error.]

The argument: even when volume estimation was accurate against the gold standard, the volume→mass conversion still fails on density, and the pipeline only worked in controlled lighting. Reading mass directly via BLE removes both failure modes — an evidence-driven pivot, not a guess.
